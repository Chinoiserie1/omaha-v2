Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 25, 2026

Omaha ("we," "our," or "the Platform") operates a Solana-based application that provides automated portfolio management through analysis of publicly available social media data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account credentials - When you sign up via Twitter (X) OAuth or email through our authentication provider (Privy), we receive your Twitter user ID, Twitter username, email address, and profile image URL.
  • Username - You choose a display username during onboarding.
  • Wallet information - An embedded Solana wallet is created for you automatically upon signup via Privy. We do not store your private keys; they are managed by Privy's secure infrastructure.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and usage data - Device type, operating system, app version, screen interactions, and crash logs collected through Expo and standard mobile analytics.
  • Blockchain data - Wallet addresses, transaction signatures, vault allocations, and on-chain activity. This data is inherently public on the Solana blockchain.

1.3 Public Data We Process

  • KOL tweet data - We ingest publicly available tweets from curated Key Opinion Leaders on X (Twitter) for the purpose of generating portfolio signals. This data includes tweet text, engagement metrics, and posting timestamps. We do not collect private or protected tweets.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide the service - Authenticate your account, create and manage your embedded wallet, display vault information, and process vault allocations.
  • Portfolio management - Use AI (Claude by Anthropic) to classify public KOL tweets and synthesize portfolio allocations, then execute on-chain rebalancing through Jupiter DEX within GLAM vaults on Solana.
  • Improve the platform - Analyze usage patterns to improve app performance, reliability, and user experience.
  • Communications - Send service-related notifications (e.g., vault rebalance events, material changes to the platform).

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in the following cases:

  • Service providers - Privy (authentication and wallet custody), Anthropic (LLM processing of public tweet data), and infrastructure providers (database hosting, RPC nodes).
  • Blockchain - Transactions executed on your behalf are recorded on the Solana blockchain and are publicly visible by nature.
  • Legal requirements - When required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the platform.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Tweet data used for portfolio signal generation is retained to maintain portfolio history and auditability. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted database connections, authenticated API endpoints with JWT verification, and secure key management through Privy. Vault operations include safety parameters such as dry-run mode, swap size limits, and price impact thresholds. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and correction - You can view and update your profile information within the app.
  • Deletion - You may request that we delete your account and personal data by emailing us at the address below.
  • Blockchain data - On-chain transaction data cannot be deleted due to the immutable nature of the Solana blockchain.

7. Children

Omaha is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes through the app or via email. Continued use of the Platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
